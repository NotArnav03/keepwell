# Q9: Render and Neon free-tier limits
Owner: Ayush · Date: 2026-11-03

## Answer (one paragraph)

Render Free is suitable for staging and static frontend previews, not for a judge-facing API that
must be immediately responsive: a free web service spins down after 15 idle minutes and the next
request can take about a minute to wake it. The allowance is 750 free instance-hours per workspace
per calendar month, shared by free services. Free Render Postgres is not a safe project database:
it is limited to 1 GB and expires 30 days after creation. Keep persistent Postgres on Neon as
planned. For a continuously available `prod-demo` API, use an always-on paid Render instance and
confirm budget/plan with the release captain before promotion; static sites may remain on Render's
free static hosting.

## Evidence

Official plan pages checked 2026-11-03:

- Render free service limitations: <https://render.com/docs/free>. Free web services spin down after
  15 minutes without inbound traffic; wake-up takes about a minute. The free web allowance is 750
  instance-hours/workspace/month. Free Render Postgres is 1 GB and expires after 30 days, then has a
  14-day upgrade window before deletion. Static sites are free.
- Render pricing: <https://render.com/pricing>. Current page lists the smallest paid service at
  $7/month (0.5 CPU / 512 MB); compute and any workspace usage are billed separately as described
  on the pricing page. Recheck before promotion because prices can change.
- Neon plans: <https://neon.com/docs/introduction/plans>, last updated 2026-10-23. Free includes
  100 projects, 10 branches/project, 100 CU-hours/project/month, 1 GB/project (20 GB account total),
  and 5 GB egress/project. Free compute scales to zero after five idle minutes and cannot be kept
  always-on. Limits can suspend compute or block writes when reached, but do not delete data.
- Resend pricing: <https://resend.com/pricing>. The free transactional plan is $0, 3,000 emails/month
  capped at 100 emails/day, with 3 domains and 30-day retention. Keep the in-app Demo Inbox as the
  judge-facing record; demo tests should not send real subscriber data.
- Sentry pricing: <https://sentry.io/pricing/>. The Developer plan is free for one user and includes
  5,000 errors/month. Treat it as a low-volume error signal, not durable logs; keep PII collection
  disabled as configured in the API.

## Impact on the design

Keep Render Free for staging and static sites only; it must not be presented as an always-on API.
Keep Neon rather than Render Postgres. Monitor Neon CU-hours, egress, storage and branch count for
the demo project; use direct (not pooled) Postgres URLs as already required by the architecture.
Use Resend only within its daily/monthly caps and keep the Demo Inbox as the judge-facing record.
Keep Sentry on the free Developer tier with PII collection disabled. This confirms the existing
staging/prod-demo split; it does not authorize a Render plan change.
