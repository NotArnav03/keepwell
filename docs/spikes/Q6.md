# Q6: Webhook latency, and does verification work on resent events?
Owner: Arnav · Date: 2026-11-03 · resend: open

## Answer (one paragraph)
**Sandbox webhooks arrive 6–16 seconds after PayPal creates the event: median 10.7 s, 90th percentile 14.9 s, from 30 deliveries.** That is much faster than the 10–75 s we had assumed. Every one of the 30 passed PayPal's `verify-webhook-signature` check with the raw body embedded verbatim, the first proof of our verification against real PayPal. All 30 were processed on the first attempt. The pipeline (queue, verify call, re-reading the subscription) took 4.6 s on average, so a change made in PayPal shows up in Keepwell about 15 seconds later. **Resend: still open.** Resending an event staging already has is answered 200 and ignored as a duplicate, which is the correct behaviour, but that never reaches verification. The real test needs an event that staging never received, for example one sent while the service is down.

## Evidence (requests and responses with secrets removed; PayPal-Debug-Id values)
Staging (`keepwell-api-staging` on Render's free plan, Neon `staging` branch), US sandbox business app and US personal buyer, 2026-11-03 08:37–08:47Z. `pnpm pool:refill 10` created 10 subscriptions on Standard ($12/month), all approved in a browser. Latency is `webhook_events.received_at` minus the event body's `create_time`.

| Event | n | min | median | p90 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BILLING.SUBSCRIPTION.CREATED` | 10 | 6.3 s | 11.5 s | 14.3 s | 14.8 s |
| `BILLING.SUBSCRIPTION.ACTIVATED` | 10 | 5.7 s | 10.0 s | 15.3 s | 15.9 s |
| `PAYMENT.SALE.COMPLETED` | 10 | 6.1 s | 8.1 s | 13.5 s | 16.0 s |
| **All** | **30** | 5.7 s | **10.7 s** | **14.9 s** | **16.0 s** |

Verification: 30 VERIFIED, 0 REJECTED. Status: 30 processed, 0 retried. Average received → processed: 4.6 s. The staging service was awake (the keep-awake ping is set); after a 15-minute sleep on the free plan, the first delivery also waits for the service to start, up to about a minute.

Side findings:
- `PAYMENT.SALE.COMPLETED` carries the subscription as `resource.billing_agreement_id` (the processor already reads it) and our `custom_id` as `resource.custom`.
- Its `summary` says `$ 12.0 USD`, while `amount.total` is `"12.00"`. Read amounts from the fields, never from the summary text.
- One `GET` of a subscription got a 503 with no debug id during the run, and the gateway's retry succeeded.

## Impact on the design (link the ADR if one was needed)
- No ADR. The design already treats webhooks as hints followed by a fresh read, so latency only changes how soon the dashboard sees a change made in PayPal: about 15 s, not "up to a minute".
- paypal-mock's comments and ARCHITECTURE §6's sequence diagram now say 6–16 s. The local mock keeps a 2 s delay, which is enough to keep the async path visible.
- The hot loop (Actions in flight, every minute) and the 30-minute sweep stay as they are: they cover missed webhooks, not slow ones.
