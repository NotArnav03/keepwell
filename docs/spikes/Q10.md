# Q10: Can Playwright approve sandbox subscriptions reliably, or is manual approval needed?
Owner: Arnav · Date: 2026-11-04 · decided: manual

## Answer (one paragraph)
**We approve pool subscriptions by hand, and Playwright isn't worth building for this project.** `pnpm pool:refill` does everything except the approval click:
- creates the subscriptions;
- lists the approval links;
- watches PayPal;
- enrols each subscription the moment it turns ACTIVE.

On 3 Nov the team approved 41 links in about an hour, taking the staging pool to 50. Automating the click would mean a browser script that signs in to `sandbox.paypal.com` with a buyer's password. A teammate would have to run it themselves, because Claude never enters credentials. It would face PayPal's anti-bot checks on its login page, and it would add a heavy dependency (Playwright plus a browser download) to save roughly an hour of clicking per 50 subscriptions. With 150 needed by 1 Dec, that's three or four such sessions.

## Evidence (requests and responses with secrets removed; PayPal-Debug-Id values)
Staging, US sandbox business app and US personal buyers, 2026-11-03:

| Run | Created | Approved and enrolled | Lost | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pnpm pool:refill 10` | 10 | 10 | 0 | 08:41–08:47Z |
| `pnpm pool:refill 40 --wait 180` | 40 | 39 | 1 | 10:52–11:29Z |
| `pnpm pool:refill 1` | 1 | 1 | 0 | 11:32Z |

- **Lost:** `I-2VLG6G1XGH2L` began answering `404 RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND / INVALID_RESOURCE_ID` (debug id `f525639248a39`) about 90 minutes after it was created. That crashed the first watcher run. Since #119 the refill drops a subscription PayPal forgets and carries on.
- **Retries:** 7 approvals (links 31–37) didn't register the first time and went through on a second attempt. The sandbox answered 503 several times during the run, and the gateway's retries covered them.
- **Approvers:** they must be US personal sandbox accounts; India accounts fail at approval (spike Q1). At about 1–2 minutes a link, splitting a batch across the team is fastest.

## Impact on the design (link the ADR if one was needed)
- No ADR. ARCHITECTURE §6.17 already allows "Playwright or a documented manual process", and this is the documented manual process: [`tools/pool-refill/README.md`](../../tools/pool-refill/README.md).
- **Refill cadence:** `pool.health` alerts below 40 available. Each refill batch is at most 50 a run, and the team approves it within a couple of hours, while the links are fresh.
- **Target:** 50+ by the feature freeze (met on 3 Nov), and 150+ by 1 Dec for judging.
- **Revisit only if** judging consumes the pool faster than about 50 a day. Even then, the cheaper fix is more approvers, not a browser bot.
