# Q2: After suspend → activate, how does billing resume?
Owner: Arnav · Date: 2026-11-01 (short pause) · long pause: running, answer due 2026-11-02

## Answer (one paragraph)
**Short pause (no billing date passes while suspended): nothing changes.** Suspending, then activating 10 seconds later, charged nothing at activation or in the minute after it. `next_billing_time` came back exactly as before (`2026-11-02T10:00:00Z`), `outstanding_balance` stayed `0.0` and `cycles_completed` stayed 1. **While SUSPENDED, PayPal leaves `next_billing_time` out of the subscription entirely**, and activation brings it back. **Long pause (a billing date passes while suspended): pending.** The subscription was suspended again before its 2026-11-02T10:00Z billing time and is activated after it, to see whether PayPal charges the missed cycle, adds it to the outstanding balance or skips it.

## Evidence (requests and responses with secrets removed; PayPal-Debug-Id values)
Sandbox, US business app + US personal buyer, plan `$1.00 / DAY` (`P-4PE44868E4826415MNLTRVKQ`), subscription `I-KN5LRF5EU8E3`, approved 2026-11-01T11:09:31Z, first $1.00 charged at approval. Recorded with `pnpm spike` (docs/spikes/README.md).

| When | status | next_billing_time | last_payment | outstanding | cycles done |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| after approval | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02T10:00:00Z | 1.0 at 11:09:31Z | 0.0 | 1 |
| suspended | SUSPENDED | *(absent)* | 1.0 at 11:09:31Z | 0.0 | 1 |
| activated (+10 s) | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02T10:00:00Z | 1.0 at 11:09:31Z | 0.0 | 1 |
| +60 s | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02T10:00:00Z | 1.0 at 11:09:31Z | 0.0 | 1 |

Transactions over the whole run: one, the $1.00 at approval (`5KJ11801403810054`). Debug ids are in each fixture file.

Side findings:
- **The first `next_billing_time` is not approval + 1 day.** The subscription was approved at 11:09Z and the next billing time is 10:00:00Z the next day: PayPal bills at a fixed time of day, not at the minute of approval. Code and copy must not assume "exactly one interval after the start".
- `outstanding_balance.value` comes back as `"0.0"`, like `last_payment` (Q1's `"1.0"`). `toMoney()` already normalises both.
- The first sandbox calls timed out (10 s) several times in a row, then worked. The gateway's retries covered single timeouts; the spike's polling loop gave up after four in a row.

## Impact on the design (link the ADR if one was needed)
- No ADR so far. The pause saga already takes the resume date from our `scheduled_resumes`, never from PayPal, so the missing `next_billing_time` while suspended changes nothing in the engine. Anything that shows "next charge" for a paused subscription must say "paused" instead of reading PayPal's field.
- paypal-mock now hides `next_billing_time` while suspended and restores it on activation (tested). What it does after a long pause stays marked "assumed" in `state.ts` until the second half of this spike.
